# Siona Biologics | Launch and supplier diligence

Status: exploratory business division of Siona Group LLC. Not an operating pharmaceutical manufacturer, licensed drug wholesaler, or approved Sinopep distributor.

## Commercial model
- Phase 1: B2B inquiries for lawful laboratory supplies, research consumables, and eligible research reagents.
- Phase 2: Supplier-qualified catalog with batch documentation and eligibility checks.
- Phase 3: Only after legal review, consider appropriate fulfillment integrations and purchasing.
- No consumer sale of unapproved injectable drugs; a "research use only" label does not make human-use marketing lawful.

## Sinopep manufacturer qualification
Contact Sinopep through a verified official corporate channel. Request:
1. Legal entity, manufacturing sites, quality certifications, and independently verifiable regulatory registrations.
2. Whether direct wholesale, authorized resale, private-label distribution, or drop shipment are available to a newly formed US business.
3. Permitted customer categories, geographic restrictions, and product-specific resale limitations.
4. Minimum order quantities, lead times, pricing tiers, shipping terms, cold-chain capabilities, returns, and recalls.
5. Representative product-specific COAs, analytical methods, lot traceability, stability data, and appropriate GMP documentation where applicable.
6. Export/import responsibilities and applicable product classifications.

Do not claim a relationship, post a catalog, take orders, or promise fulfillment without written authorization and counsel review.

## Legal and brand gates
- Trademark counsel to assess Siona Biologics, including similarity to Sionna Therapeutics.
- Counsel to distinguish general research reagents from drug APIs, finished drugs, biologics, and regulated cosmetics.
- Review US federal/state import, labeling, advertising, sales, tax, and distribution requirements.
- Confirm product-specific restrictions, buyer qualification, and acceptable payment processor terms.
- Obtain appropriate product liability and general liability coverage before selling.
