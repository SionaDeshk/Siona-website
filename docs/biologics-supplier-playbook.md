# Siona Biologics | Supplier Development Playbook
Status: Internal working draft. No suppliers approved, no products for sale.

## First commercial wedge
Start with institutional research consumables and non-clinical analytical reagents after category-by-category legal review. Do **not** market unapproved peptides for human use, imply that research-use-only labels legalize intended human use, or promise clinical-grade products without substantiation.

## Qualification gates (record evidence, not claims)
1. **Identity:** legal company name, manufacturing entity, country, business registration, named representative, factory vs distributor, beneficial ownership screening.
2. **Regulatory/product classification:** exact SKU, intended use, destination jurisdiction, import/export restrictions, labeling, required registrations, whether API/drug/device/other. Obtain counsel or regulatory specialist review for borderline categories.
3. **Quality:** quality agreement if applicable, batch-specific COA, test methods and numerical results, independent lab verification plan, impurity profile, storage and stability, deviations, complaints, recalls, change control, audit rights.
4. **Traceability:** original manufacturer, lot, chain of custody, transport conditions, shipping documents, certificates and retention rules.
5. **Commercial:** MOQ, tiered pricing, Incoterms, payment terms, lead time, replacement terms, sample policy, customs broker and landed cost.
6. **Pilot:** choose a low-risk permitted SKU; validate with a qualified institutional buyer before inventory commitment; no purchases until compliance, supplier, customer and economics gates pass.

## Outreach stages
Identified → Contacted → Responded → Documents requested → Compliance review → Sample evaluation → Negotiation → Approved / Rejected.
A response is not an agreement. A COA alone is not supplier approval.

## Next seven actions
- Confirm EIN and legal operating details before contracting.
- Build a shortlist of 10 legitimate manufacturers and distributors in permitted research supply categories.
- Interview five laboratory procurement professionals about bottlenecks and vendor onboarding.
- Send qualification questionnaire to responding suppliers.
- Map landed cost and fulfillment for two low-risk candidate categories.
- Identify an independent analytical testing partner and a regulatory consultant.
- Prepare a supplier quality agreement template for legal review.

## Regulatory starting points
FDA ICH Q7: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q7-good-manufacturing-practice-guidance-active-pharmaceutical-ingredients-guidance-industry
FDA ICH Q7 Q&A: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/q7-good-manufacturing-practice-guidance-active-pharmaceutical-ingredients-questions-and-answers

ICH Q7 governs APIs within its scope; do not apply it indiscriminately to every research consumable.
